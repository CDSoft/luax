# A note on freedom

The freedom to run, study, modify, and share software - the very freedoms
this license grants you - is inseparable from freedom of thought, freedom
of expression, and the pluralism of democratic societies.
Fascism, in all its forms, is the negation of these freedoms: it demands
conformity of thought, silences dissent, and persecutes those who differ.

By its very design, this software is therefore incompatible with fascist,
Nazi, and supremacist ideologies.
Not because its license forbids them - it does not and cannot, as that
would make it non-free - but because these ideologies are fundamentally
at odds with the values this project is built on.

If you use this software to promote fascism, you are not a member of
this community, whatever the license allows.
We invite you to reflect on the contradiction of building anything on
a foundation of freedom you would deny to others.
